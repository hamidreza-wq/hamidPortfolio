## What it does

Besides the built-in sections (home, about, work, craft, approach and contact), you can add your own: **Experience**, **Education**, **Services**, **Testimonials** or **Custom** plain-text sections.

## Adding a section

1. Choose the **section type** and give it a **navigation label** and **title**.
2. Add an optional subtitle, description, image and background.
3. Add **structured items** as a list. Each item has a title, and an optional subtitle and body. Up to 20 items are allowed.
4. Save. New sections start **hidden**, so check them before you make them visible.

## Testimonials sections

A section of type **Testimonials** fills itself with every approved testimonial from the [Testimonials](/admin/testimonials) page, featured ones first, followed by any items you typed in the section itself.

## Good to know

- Sections are plain text only. HTML isn't allowed, which keeps the site safe.
- Built-in sections are edited in the [live site editor](/admin/site-editor), not here.
- Hidden sections are left out of the menu automatically.
- Don't invent details or testimonials. Everything shown should be true and approved by the people involved.
