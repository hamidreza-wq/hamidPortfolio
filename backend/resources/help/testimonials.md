## What it does

Collect what clients say about your work, review it, and publish only what you approve. Approved testimonials appear in any visible section of type **Testimonials**.

## Getting testimonials

**From clients.** Open the client form on the Testimonials page, then send the link to the people you've worked with. The form is **closed** until you open it. Clients give their name, their words, an optional role, company and rating, and agree to publication. Their email address stays private.

**Received elsewhere.** Use **Add a testimonial yourself** for praise from emails or LinkedIn. Only add words the person actually wrote, with their permission.

## Reviewing

Everything from the form arrives under **Waiting for review** and stays hidden until you act.

1. Read it, and check the private email address if anything looks unfamiliar.
2. **Approve and publish**, **Reject**, or send it **Back to review**.
3. **Edit** to fix typos, set the order, or **feature** it so it shows first.

## Showing them on the site

Add a section of type **Testimonials** on the [Sections](/admin/sections) page. The Testimonials page warns you when there isn't one.

## Good to know

- Rejected submissions are deleted automatically after 90 days.
- Deleting a testimonial also deletes the private email address it held.
- If someone asks for their testimonial to be removed, delete it, or use [Privacy](/admin/help/privacy) data requests to remove everything linked to their email address.
- Search engines are told not to list the client form.
