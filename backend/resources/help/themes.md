## What it does

A theme is the whole look of the site in one go: the five colours, the body and heading fonts, content width, section spacing, card corners, interface motion and the particle field. Applying a theme never changes your content.

## Trying a theme

1. Press **Preview** on any theme. The preview on the right shows your real site in that theme. Nothing is published.
2. Press **Apply** to make it live.

The look you replace is saved in [Content history](/admin/revisions), so you can always go back. **Restore default** on *Cosmic* returns to the original look.

## Making your own

- **Save as a theme** captures the look that's live now.
- **Duplicate to edit** copies a ready-made theme into *Your themes*.
- **Edit** a saved theme to change every value, then **Preview these values** before saving.
- **Export** downloads a theme as a small file. **Import** loads a file exported from this dashboard, on this site or another.

## Good to know

> All ready-made themes are dark. The site's stylesheet is written for dark backgrounds, so a light background would make some text unreadable.

- Older presets that only stored colours keep your current fonts and layout when applied. They're marked *Colours only*.
- Imported files are checked strictly. Unknown settings are ignored, and a theme can only use fonts uploaded to this site.
- For small adjustments to one colour or font, the [live site editor](/admin/site-editor) is quicker.
