## What it does

Messages sent through the contact form on your portfolio arrive here. If an email address is configured on the server, a copy is also emailed to you.

## Working through messages

- **Search** by name, email address or subject, and **filter** by status.
- Set each message to **Unread**, **Read** or **Archived** to keep track.
- Add a **private note**. Only you see it.
- **Delete** removes a message permanently.

## Spam protection

The form rejects messages sent too quickly to have been typed by a person, catches bots with a hidden field, and limits how many messages one person can send.

## Good to know

- Messages contain personal information. Old ones can be deleted automatically after a period you choose on the [Privacy](/admin/privacy) page.
- If someone asks for a copy of their data, or for it to be deleted, use **Data requests** on the [Privacy](/admin/help/privacy) page. It finds everything linked to their email address.
