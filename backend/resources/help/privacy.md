## What it does

The Privacy page shows what personal information the site holds, lets you decide how long to keep it, and helps you answer people who ask about their data.

> These tools help you look after personal data, but they don't make the site legally compliant on their own. If you're unsure about your obligations, check with someone qualified.

## Personal data on this site

A table lists each kind of personal data (contact messages, testimonials, activity-log IP addresses, admin sessions, pageview counts and backups) with how many records there are and how long they're kept.

## Retention

Choose how long to keep:

- **Contact messages**: deleted automatically after 90 days, 6 months, 1 year or 2 years, or kept until you delete them.
- **Activity-log IP addresses**: cleared after a period. The log entries themselves stay.

Both start as *keep*, so nothing is deleted until you choose. The clean-up runs once a day, and the public privacy notice states whatever you choose.

## Answering a data request

When someone asks what you hold about them, or asks you to delete it:

1. Check the request really comes from them. For example, reply to the email address it came from.
2. Enter their email address under **Data requests** and press **Find**.
3. **Download a copy** to send them everything held, or
4. **Erase this person's data** to delete their messages and testimonials. You'll need your password, and this can't be undone.

The activity log records that a request was handled, without storing the email address.

## Good to know

- Older backups keep erased data until newer backups replace them, about two weeks with daily backups.
- Notification emails already in your mailbox have to be deleted there.
- Google Analytics data is managed in your Google account.
