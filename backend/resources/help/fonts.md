## What it does

Upload web fonts you're allowed to use, then choose them for headings or body text in the [live site editor](/admin/site-editor) or in a [theme](/admin/themes).

## Uploading a font

1. Choose a **WOFF2**, **WOFF**, **TTF** or **OTF** file, up to 2 MB. WOFF2 is the smallest and best for websites.
2. Give it a **family label**, and set its **weight** and **style**. Upload each weight or style as a separate file.
3. Add where it came from and the licence details.
4. Tick that you have the right to publish and embed it, then upload.

## Good to know

> Only upload fonts whose licence allows web embedding. Many desktop font licences don't.

- Files are checked to be real fonts of the type their name says.
- A font in use by the site can't be deleted.
- The built-in choices (Inter, System UI, Georgia and Monospace) need no upload.
