## What it does

Search looks through the dashboard itself and everything you manage in it: dashboard pages, projects, sections, site settings, skills, testimonials, social links, menus, media, fonts, contact messages and these help articles.

## Quick search

1. Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac) on any page, or click **Search the dashboard** in the top bar.
2. Start typing. Results appear after two characters, grouped by type.
3. Use the **↑** and **↓** arrow keys to move, and **Enter** to open the highlighted result. **Esc** closes the search.

Each result opens the place where that item is edited, for example the exact project form or the field in the live editor.

## Full results

Choose **All results** at the bottom of the quick search, or open [Search](/admin/search) directly. There you can narrow the results to one type, for example only contact messages, and see more matches for each group.

## Good to know

- Dashboard pages are matched on everyday words as well as their names: "2fa" finds Security, "colours" finds the live editor, and "restore" finds Backups.
- Symbols such as `%` and `_` are searched as ordinary characters.
- Search is limited to 120 searches a minute, which normal typing never reaches.
