## What it does

The Menus page controls the links in the site's **header** and **footer**.

## Automatic or custom header

While the header menu is **automatic**, it lists every visible section that has *Show in navigation* ticked, in section order. That's how the site has always worked.

Press **Customise the header menu** to take control. The custom menu starts as an exact copy of what visitors currently see, so nothing jumps around. From then on, sections no longer add themselves to the menu. **Go back to automatic** deletes the custom menu and returns to the old behaviour.

## Adding an item

1. Open **Add an item** under the menu.
2. Choose what it **links to**:
   - a section of the home page
   - a project page
   - a site page (top of the page, privacy notice or testimonial form)
   - another website or an email address
3. Give it a label, or leave the label blank to use the section, project or page name. Links to other websites need a label.
4. Optionally make it **open in a new tab**, or, in the header, **show it as a button**.

Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to change the order, and **Edit** to change or remove an item.

## No broken links

An item pointing at a hidden section, an unpublished project or a closed testimonial form is left out automatically, and the Menus page shows why it's hidden. It comes back as soon as the target is visible again.

## Good to know

- Other websites must use `https://`. Email links use `mailto:you@example.com`.
- The footer menu sits beside the **Privacy** link, which is always shown.
- Your [social links](/admin/help/social-links) appear after the menu items.
- Dropdowns aren't offered: on a one-page site they mostly make the phone menu harder to use.
