## What it does

The Analytics page shows how people find and use your site, from three sources that are deliberately kept separate:

- **Pageview counts** recorded by the site itself: page loads over 7 and 30 days, daily totals, the most viewed pages and the websites that sent visitors. They don't use cookies, and don't store IP addresses, full referrer addresses or browser details.
- **Google Analytics**, which runs only for visitors who choose *Allow analytics* in the cookie banner. Detailed reports are in your Google Analytics account.
- **Google Search Console**: clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position in Google search, once connected.

## Connecting Search Console

1. Press **Connect Google Search Console** and sign in with the Google account that owns the property.
2. Allow read-only access.
3. If Google later revokes access, the page explains why and offers **Reconnect**.

## Good to know

- Pageview counts are page loads, not unique visitors. One person viewing three pages counts as three.
- Search Console data arrives about three days late. That's normal.
- Pageview records are deleted after 90 days.
