## What it does

Every time you publish site changes, save a project, apply a theme or restore something, the version being replaced is saved here first. You can look back through those versions and restore any of them.

**Site revisions** cover the profile, SEO settings, theme, sections and skills. **Project revisions** cover a whole project.

## Restoring a revision

1. Find the revision by its label and date.
2. Tick **I understand this replaces and publishes the content**.
3. Press **Restore revision**.

> Restoring publishes that version immediately. The content it replaces is saved as a new revision, so a restore can itself be undone.

## Good to know

- Images are referenced, not copied. Restoring brings back which image was chosen, but not an image file you have since deleted. Images that are used in saved history can't be deleted.
- Contact messages, testimonials, menus and social links aren't part of content history. For a full safety net, use [Backups](/admin/backups).
