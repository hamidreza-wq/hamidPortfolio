## What it does

Notifications collect everything that needs your attention in one place, in seven categories:

- **Security**: a burst of failed sign-ins, two-factor sign-in being turned off, or a recovery code being used.
- **Site health**: red checks on the [Health](/admin/health) page, such as errors, low disk space, an expiring certificate or a stopped scheduler.
- **Backups** that fail.
- **Updates**: new releases, and failed updates or deployments.
- **Messages**: new contact messages, and testimonials waiting for review.
- **SEO** problems and broken pages.
- **GitHub** repositories to review.

The same problem never appears twice. A notification reopens only if it changes, or comes back after being fixed, and most clear themselves once the problem is gone. For example, the messages notification clears when you've read every message.

## Working with notifications

- Use the category buttons, and the **Show** filter, to see only what you need: open items, unread only, or everything, including resolved items.
- **Open related page** takes you to the page where the problem is fixed.
- **Mark read** clears an item from your unread count. **Mark all read** does it for everything shown.
- **Snooze 24 hours** hides it from the badge for a day. **Dismiss** hides it until it changes or comes back.

## Emails

Under **What you're told about, and how**:

- Untick categories you don't want in your unread badge and emails. They still appear on the page.
- **Email me straight away about high-priority alerts** sends an email when something serious happens, such as a failed backup or two-factor being turned off. Alerts are grouped, with at most one email every 15 minutes, and a problem that's only reworded doesn't email you again.
- **Summary email** sends a daily or weekly roundup.

> Emails need the server's mail settings and its scheduler. They never contain message text, the names of people who wrote to you, or passwords: only what happened, and a link back here.
