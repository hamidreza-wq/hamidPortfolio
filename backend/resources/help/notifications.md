## What it does

Notifications collect the things that need your attention in one place: new releases and failed updates, GitHub repositories to review, SEO problems, failed backups and critical health checks.

## Working with notifications

- **Mark read** clears it from your unread count.
- **Snooze 24 hours** hides the badge for a day.
- **Dismiss** hides it until the problem changes or comes back.

The same warning never appears twice. It reopens only if something changes.

## Email digests

Under **Delivery preferences**, choose which categories you care about, and whether to get a daily or weekly email summary. Email only works once the server's mail settings and the scheduler are configured. Digests never include private messages or passwords.
